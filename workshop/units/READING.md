# A qualitative reading, not a claim

Status: a reading. Nothing here is checked, and nothing here is built.

The ontological framing offered in conversation treats synthesis as the frame, and treats agents as oriented toward good. Two parts of it are not carried into this repo as formal claims:
- the claim that adversarial or malware perspectives should be eliminated, since that is a policy choice, not a checkable statement;
- the claim that evil is debunked, which needs a definition of evil that would have to be argued, not asserted.

Readings that can be stated and later tested:
- Reading: agents can be designed so that their outputs are checked against a stated goal before they are used.
- Reading: a dialectic can be modelled as two opposed stances with a synthesis, without declaring either side void.

Open: what the goal of an agent is, stated so it can be refuted.
