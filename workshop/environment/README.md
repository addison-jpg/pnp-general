# The environmental side, from the individual up

Status: an index of work already in the repo. It adds no new claim. Each layer's label is the label its own file gives.

## Layers, from the individual upward

1. **Individual: a toy model of recovery under repeated harm.** `workshop/recovery/`. Three states, both-way moves. `model.ath`: 3 true, 0 false. Describes no person. Makes no claim that psychosis or harm is a method. Label: checked-finite, for the toy graph only.

2. **Social structure: nine core Marxist concepts.** `culture/marx/MARXISM.md`. A concept map, eight labelled analogy and two open (base and superstructure, dialectic). No claim that the theory is true or false. Label: analogy and open.

3. **Information environment: prediction markets and public opinion.** `workshop/kalshi_review/`. Literature review in four parts. No peer-reviewed study found that markets change public opinion. Polls disagree, and the most favourable one is company-commissioned. Panopticon and sign readings are labelled analogy. Labels: published sources, report, industry-published, analogy.
