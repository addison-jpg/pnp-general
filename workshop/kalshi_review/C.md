## What the publisher has an interest in

- **Kalshi:** Its calibration and accuracy papers support its pitch as a forecasting tool. Its inflation claim is a comparison it made against Wall Street forecasts. Both are self-published, with no independent review shown.
- **Coalition for Prediction Markets:** Members are trading platforms and brokers. Per the search summary, its stated aims are federal rules, including countering state regulators. Its poll-beating claim supports that policy push.
- **American Gaming Association:** It is a competing gambling-industry trade group. Its poll favors treating prediction markets as gambling.
