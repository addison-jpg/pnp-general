## Conflicts

- **Axis Research poll, commissioned by Kalshi.** Kalshi is the company whose markets the poll asks about. Critics cite the sponsor's stake.
- No other poll in this set is shown as company-funded. The Brennan Center funder was not found, so this cannot be ruled out.
