# "Ideology does not exist properly": three readings and what each breaks

Status: question and hypotheses only. Nothing is checked; no asteroid run was made. Sources are search results, not opened texts. Links to Marxism are labelled **analogy**.

## Question

If "ideology" does not pick out a coherent kind, which claims in this repo fail, and which stand?

## Hypotheses (all open)

**E1 (no coherent kind).** In mass publics, beliefs are not organised into constrained systems, so "ideology" names no stable kind there.
- Evidence for: Converse (1964), "The Nature of Belief Systems in Mass Publics": low constraint in the mass public, higher in elites. Search summary only.
- Evidence against: later reassessments; see Kulinski and Peyton (2007), which describes three revisions. Summary only.
- Refuted by: survey data showing issue positions tightly structured across the public, measured with a method that is not itself the cause of the structure.
- Limit: concerns mass publics. It does not cover elites.

**E2 (self-undermining; Mannheim, from memory).** If all thought is ideological, the claim "all thought is ideological" is too. If no thought is, ideology critique has no object.
- Refuted by: a definition of "ideology" that separates the critic's claim from the claims criticised.

**E3 (postmodern; Lyotard on grand narratives, from memory).** No hypothesis stated yet.
- Needs: an exact statement, then a refutation condition.

## What it breaks, and what it does not

- **Breaks if E1 holds:** the "ideology" row of `culture/marx/MARXISM.md`; A1 in `workshop/kalshi_marx/README.md` (its ideology half); any claim of the form "every ideology shares one property", since the set "every ideology" has no definition.
- **Stands:** H1, H2, D1 to D3 and A2 to A4 in `workshop/kalshi_marx/README.md`. They do not use the word.
- **Also open:** "we don't know what we don't know" blocks asserting "ideology does not exist" as fact, so the claim stays a hypothesis.

- That ideology exists or does not exist.
- That any ideology is good, bad or equal to another in consequence. Shared structure does not mean shared harm.
