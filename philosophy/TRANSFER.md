# Transfer, philosophically

The philosophical content of `core/ontology/TRANSFER.md`: an extension that solves a subclass is a claim about where the subclass sits in the larger field. It is a reading of structure, not evidence that the larger field is correct. Barriers (relativization, natural proofs, algebrization) show that some extensions cannot reach what they hope to, which is a limit on method, not a failure of the subject.
