# The asteroid logo

The logo is the word `4STeroid`: `4ST` in the hand and typing style of Terezi Pyrope (capitals, A written as 4), in teal, and `eroid` in the hand of Vriska Serket, in blue. This is the only place these styles are used; text, documentation and terminal output keep their ordinary fonts.

The letters are drawn as outlines (`<path>`), so nobody needs a font installed to see them, and no font file is shipped in this repository.

Colours: the `logo` role (the `4ST` half) and the `logo_alt` role (the `eroid` half) in `palette.json`, light and dark. To change them, edit those values and run:

    python3 asteroid/tools/make_logo.py --terezi PATH/terezzzzzzz.ttf --vriska PATH/Arachnidsgrip1.otf

## Credit

- Lettering of `4ST`: the font **terezzzzzzz**, a fan-made handwriting font in the style of Terezi Pyrope, made with MyScriptFont.com (FontForge, 20 August 2013). Designer not named in the font.
- Lettering of `eroid`: the font **Arachnidsgrip1**, a fan-made handwriting font in the style of Vriska Serket, made with Calligraphr (2 September 2021). Designer not named in the font.
- Terezi Pyrope and Vriska Serket are characters of *Homestuck* by Andrew Hussie.
- A shout-out to the *Homestuck: Beyond Canon* team. Addison loved the existentialist Dirk monologues.

If you made either font and want a different credit, a link, or the logo removed, open an issue.

## Variant

`logo-variant-dark.svg`: the same lettering filled with one teal-to-blue gradient, on a dark rounded panel, with small faint ASCII faces scattered behind it. Made by hand from `logo-dark.svg`; it is not regenerated by `make_logo.py`.
