# Marxism: a concept map (core concepts only)

## Scope

This map covers nine core concepts of Marx and Engels: historical materialism, base and superstructure, class, surplus value, alienation, mode of production, dialectic, ideology, and commodity fetishism. It does not cover later schools, commentary, or the history of the movement.

## Concepts

Labels: "analogy" is a reading, with no check. "open" is a question this repo does not settle, or a meaning this map is unsure of.

| concept | one-line meaning in plain words | label | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| historical materialism | history changes mainly through how people produce their livelihood, and that shapes the rest of social life | analogy | Marx and Engels, standard texts |
| base and superstructure | the economic relations (base) shape law, politics and ideas (superstructure); how strong that link is, is disputed | open | Marx and Engels, standard texts |
| class | groups defined by their place in production, such as owners of the means of production and sellers of labour power | analogy | Marx and Engels, standard texts |
| surplus value | the value workers create beyond the value of their wages, which the owners of capital take | analogy | Marx and Engels, standard texts |
| alienation | under wage labour, workers are estranged from their product, their activity, their species-nature and each other | analogy | Marx and Engels, standard texts |
| mode of production | a way of organising production, defined by productive forces and the relations between people in production | analogy | Marx and Engels, standard texts |
| dialectic | a method that reads change through contradictions; the exact sense, and how Hegel's form was taken over, is unsettled here | open | Marx and Engels, standard texts |
| ideology | ideas that serve a ruling class's interests and present them as natural or universal | analogy | Marx and Engels, standard texts |
| commodity fetishism | market relations between things appear as relations between things, hiding the social labour behind them | analogy | Marx and Engels, standard texts |

## Not claimed

This map makes no claim that Marxist theory is true or false, and no claim about any political program.

## Link to this repo

The repo's bus and board structures are not Marxist, and no structural analogy between them and any concept above is checked.
